Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ONEBOX handles information when you use the website.

Information you provide

Some tools may process text, numbers, images, PDFs or other files that you choose to provide. Many tools are designed to process data inside your browser. Where a tool uses a third-party service or library, data handling may also be subject to that provider's policies.

Analytics and advertising

ONEBOX may use analytics and advertising services in the future. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure traffic, improve the website, prevent abuse, and show relevant ads.

Third-party services

Certain features may load third-party resources. For example, PDF functionality may use external JavaScript libraries and QR features may use an external QR service. Those providers may receive technical information necessary to deliver their service.

Data security

Do not upload highly sensitive personal, financial or confidential information unless a tool clearly states how it is handled and you are comfortable using it.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when ONEBOX adds new features, analytics, advertising or account functionality.
